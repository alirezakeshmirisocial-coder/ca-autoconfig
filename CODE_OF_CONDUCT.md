# Community code of conduct

CA AutoConfig welcomes careful experimentation and disagreement backed by evidence. Participation must remain safe, respectful, and useful to people with different hardware, budgets, languages, and levels of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and benchmark methods without attacking the person presenting them.
- State uncertainty, hardware limitations, sponsorships, and conflicts of interest.
- Protect personal data found in logs, screenshots, configuration, and benchmark submissions.
- Respect community promotion rules and do not ask others to manipulate stars, votes, or rankings.
- Give maintainers and reporters enough information to reproduce a result.
- Accept a safety-first decision when a proposed feature could damage hardware or weaken a privilege boundary.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate misinformation, doxxing, publication of secrets or biometric data, unsafe instructions presented as verified advice, spam, brigading, and attempts to purchase or automate community endorsements are not permitted.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions, temporarily limit participation, or permanently ban participants when necessary to protect the project and community. Enforcement decisions should be proportional, documented privately, and avoid exposing a reporter's personal information.

Report conduct problems privately to the maintainer. Reports will be reviewed in good faith, and retaliation against reporters is prohibited.
